**What:** writes LinkedIn invite notes, first messages and follow-ups in your voice: short, one true fact, no pitch, nothing invented.
**Install:** Claude: `npx dewx skills install` (or copy this folder to `~/.claude/skills/`, or zip it and upload in Claude Settings, Skills). ChatGPT: new GPT, paste `SKILL.md` into Instructions. Any other AI: paste `SKILL.md` at the start of the chat.
**Example:** "Write an m1 for Priya, partner at a law firm. Her post says they close the books in four days instead of ten. I'm Sam, I do bookkeeping for law firms."
